## 1. Product definition

MAINSTREET is a cozy town investing game for children. Kids own little pieces of six town shops and a Town Basket Fund, keep money for soon in a Savings Jar, make a plan for a concrete goal, and watch what time, news and their choices change. Six short story chapters introduce one money idea each, then unlock a 20-year free play. The one-line description: **"Own little pieces of a town. Make a plan. See what time changes."**

The teaching claim is: *match your money to your goal, spread it out, and understand that outcomes are uncertain.* It is not "always stay invested." Spending on a planned goal, keeping cash for something soon, and selling a damaged business can all be sensible, and the game treats them that way.

**Audience:** primary ages 8 to 11, with a Guided presentation that supports 7+. Two presentation levels (Guided, Explorer) change information density only. Neither changes market luck, honesty, or scoring. The game never asks for age, name, school or any personal fact.

**Non-negotiables** (unchanged from v1; Astra's review preserved them)

- Free. No ads, in-app purchases, real money or real crypto anywhere.
- No player communication: no chat, trading or bartering between players, accounts, login or leaderboards.
- Offline after first load. No network requests during play. Saves live on the device only.
- A fictional market. No real tickers, companies or prices.
- One authored `index.html` in vanilla HTML/CSS/JS, mobile-first, no build step, plus `sw.js`, `manifest.webmanifest` and icons.
- Never celebrate a single trade. Celebrate finishing a chapter and understanding a comparison.
- No loot boxes, spin wheels, rarity, streaks, daily-login pressure, timers, leverage, shorting or "act now" prompts.
- Calm presentation during drops: no flashing red, no alarm sounds, Buy and Sell equally available.
- Raw return is shown but never drives the score.
- Every game word maps to the real one; plain financial words are used in action labels.

**Launch (v1.0) includes:** six chapters, free play with scenario presets, six shops, Town Basket Fund, Savings Jar with Soon and Buffer earmarks, dividends with optional reinvestment, practice contributions, one life-cost type, one company closure variant, inflation (chapter 6 and free play), a matched fee lab, two benchmark plans, the time machine comparison, scrapbook and decorations, Guided/Explorer, optional local read-aloud, export/import of saves.

**Launch excludes** (backlog, section 15): the Glow Slime fad asset and any crypto-flavored content, Pro Picker Fund, named rival characters, share cards and replay links, daily seeded scenario, Family Night mode, bonds, standalone ORCHARD app, Play Store packaging.

**Platform:** Lucid Winds arcade PWA first (Test Lab, then the public arcade). Play Store only after a separate packaging and policy pass.

## 4. Assets and market model

Six shops, one fund, one jar. Nothing else at launch. The model is a toy: a monthly simple-return random walk with three market moods and sector-correlated noise. The parameters below are the starting tuning candidate from v1; section 13 says how they get recalibrated. Do not describe them as real-world return expectations anywhere in the product.

### Assets

| ID | Name | Sector | Start price (coins) | Price drift, yearly | Volatility, yearly | Dividend yield, yearly | Market loading `mkt` | Sector loading `sec` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LEMN | Lemon Lane | Food | 8.00 | 5.0% | 28% | 2.0% | 0.40 | 0.40 |
| BAKE | Rise & Shine Bakery | Food | 10.00 | 4.5% | 14% | 3.5% | 0.45 | 0.35 |
| TOYS | Toy Box | Fun | 12.00 | 6.0% | 24% | 1.5% | 0.55 | 0.40 |
| BIKE | Spoke Bikes | Outdoor | 15.00 | 5.5% | 20% | 2.5% | 0.50 | 0.35 |
| PAWS | Paws Pet Care | Care | 9.00 | 5.0% | 16% | 2.5% | 0.40 | 0.30 |
| ROBO | Robo Lab | Tech | 20.00 | 9.0% | 36% | 0% | 0.55 | 0.35 |

- **Drift excludes dividends** and is annualized. Monthly drift = yearly ÷ 12; monthly volatility = yearly ÷ 3.4641016151377544 (√12, stored as a literal). Total expected return = drift + yield.
- `mkt` and `sec` are noise loadings in the toy model, not CAPM betas. Own-noise loading `own = √(1 − mkt² − sec²)`, precomputed and stored as a literal per asset.
- **BASKET, Town Basket Fund:** holds all six shops at equal target weight, rebalanced each January, expense 0.2% a year. Its card says: "Six shops in one town. Spread out, but still one town." It keeps and reinvests the shops' dividends inside itself and pays none out. Start NAV 10.00 per unit. In chapter 6 only, two baskets exist: BASKET\_A (0.2%) and BASKET\_B (1.5%) with identical holdings.
- **JAR, Savings Jar:** cash with earmarks **Soon** (goal money) and **Buffer** (for surprise costs). Interest is a scenario setting (0% or 2%), accrued monthly. The wallet is a third cash balance for everyday use. Wallet plus Jar is all cash; both count in net worth.

### Market moods (regime chain)

Checked once per month. Drift added is scaled by `mkt ÷ 0.5` per asset. Verified stationary mix: 67.6% Normal, 27.9% Boom, 4.5% Slump.

| Mood | Drift added, yearly | Volatility × | Inflation, yearly (ch. 6 and free play) | Monthly chance of switching |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Normal | −1.15% | 1.0 | 2.5% | to Boom 1.0%, to Slump 0.5% |
| Boom | +6.0% | 0.8 | 3.5% | to Normal 2.5%, to Slump 0.4% |
| Slump | −20.0% | 1.5 | 1.5% | to Normal 7.0%, to Boom 3.0% |

Chapters override the chain: `off` (Normal only, chapters 2 and 3), `noSlump` (chapter 4), `authored` (chapter 5's scripted slump window), `full` (chapter 6, free play).

### Monthly shock

For each asset, `z = mkt·z_market + sec·z_sector + own·z_own`, each `z` an Irwin-Hall draw (sum of six uniforms, centered, scaled by 1.4142135623730951). Monthly gross price factor `G = 1 + driftM + moodDriftM + volM·volMul·z + eventShock`, floored at 0.4. Noise is bounded at about ±4.2 standard deviations; authored stress presets, not the noise, supply rare crashes.

### Dividends

Paid in months 3, 6, 9 and 12 of each year when the chapter has dividends on. Per share, `D = openingPrice × yield ÷ 4`. The ex-dividend price is `P_next = openingPrice × G − D`, which is always positive given the floor and yields under 1% a quarter. Holdings are snapshotted before the market advances (section 5). Direct holdings receive cash to the wallet; with Reinvest on, the cash buys shares at the ex-dividend price in the same tick. E18 can halve or suspend a shop's yield for 12 months. A shop pays nothing in a month its price factor fell below 0.7 (a bad month suspends the quarter's payment).

### Fund mechanics

The Basket tracks internal holdings per unit, `h_i` shares of each shop, plus internal cash. `NAV = Σ h_i · P_i + cash`. Each month: internal dividends land in cash and buy shares pro rata at ex prices; the fee factor `(1 − expense ÷ 12)` scales all `h_i`; in January, holdings are reset to equal weights. Look-through exposure for the Spread score is `h_i · P_i ÷ NAV`. NAV stays a float internally and is quantized to centi-coins only for quotes, so a 0.2% fee is never rounded away.

### Company closure (E19)

At the announcement month: price drops to a liquidation value of 25% to 50% of the prior price (seeded), trading in that shop is suspended, the storefront shows a closing notice. Next month: every holder receives the liquidation value per share as cash, the holding is removed, and the Basket moves its proceeds equally into the remaining five shops with no fee. The shop never reopens in that run. Free play reports it in the recap as a permanent loss, different from a slump.

### Inflation

A cumulative price index starts at 1.000 and multiplies monthly by `(1 + moodInflation ÷ 12)` when inflation is on. Real value = nominal ÷ index, both in month-0 coins. The Jar balance never shrinks in coins; the "what it can buy" view shows the real value beside it.

## 5. Ledger contract

This section is locked. The UI, the replay, the benchmarks, the harness and the fixtures all use exactly this order and these units. If anything here changes, update the fixtures in the same commit.

### Units and rounding

| Quantity | Stored as | Scale | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Money (wallet, Jar earmarks, ledger totals) | Integer `cc` | 100 cc = 1 coin | Guided shows whole coins ("about 24 coins" when there are cents). Explorer shows two decimals |
| Shares and fund units | Integer `msh` | 1,000 msh = 1 share | "Part of a share" in Guided; exact quantity in details |
| Quoted price or NAV | Integer `cc` per share |  | `quote = Math.round(priceFloat × 100)`, minimum 1 cc |
| Internal price, NAV, index, fee residue | Float |  | Only inside the engine; quantized once at the ledger boundary |

- Position value `= floor(msh × quote ÷ 1000)` cc, computed with BigInt: `Number(BigInt(msh) * BigInt(quote) / 1000n)`.
- Buy with `X` cc: `msh = floor(X × 1000 ÷ quote)`; cash spent is exactly `X`. Whole-share buys spend `quote × n`.
- Sell `n` msh: proceeds `= floor(n × quote ÷ 1000)` cc.
- Rounding loss per transaction is under 1 cc. The conservation test tolerance is 1 cc per transaction.
- Dividend to a holder `= floor(msh × D_cc ÷ 1000)` where `D_cc = Math.round(openingQuote × yield ÷ 4)`.
- Caps: any balance or value above 10¹² cc (10 billion coins) rejects the action with a structured error. All stored integers must satisfy `Number.isSafeInteger`.
- A transaction can never produce a negative wallet, negative earmark, negative holding, NaN or Infinity. `validateAction` returns the error before `applyAction` is called.

### Tick order (one month)

1. **Execute** the queued user actions at the frozen quotes. Reject overspend and oversell.
2. **Contribute:** add this month's practice coins (E22) to the wallet, then route them per the contribution plan. Only money that exists is routed.
3. **Snapshot** holdings entitled to this month's dividends.
4. **Advance the market:** step the mood chain; apply scheduled event shocks; compute each shop's `G`; compute `P_next` (ex-dividend when a payment is due); step the price index; accrue Jar interest.
5. **Distribute:** pay dividends on the snapshot once; credit cash; if Reinvest is on, buy shares at the ex-dividend quote.
6. **Fund housekeeping:** reinvest the Basket's internal dividends, apply its monthly fee factor, run the January rebalance. Never a child trade; never a fee on the child.
7. **Decisions:** process closure payouts; queue life costs (E20), goal day (E21) and any card with `decisionRequired`. If the queue is not empty, stop the time advance with time frozen and present them in order. Background cards go to the digest.
8. **Checkpoint:** update ledger totals, append the month to history, save, render.

A multi-month advance ("Next year") runs this loop up to 12 times and stops early at step 7 when a decision is queued. "Continue year" resumes from the next month. Nothing in the UI can run step 4 on its own.

### Ledger totals and the money identity

At every checkpoint the engine maintains `contributionsIn`, `dividendsCash`, `jarInterest`, `goalSpending`, `costSpending`, `fundFeesEstimate` (disclosed, not deducted again) and `netWorth = wallet + jar + Σ positionValues`. The identity tested at every tick:

```
netWorth_t − netWorth_{t−1} = contributionsIn + jarInterest + marketChange − goalSpending − costSpending
```

where `marketChange` is the residual and includes price moves, dividends (cash or reinvested) and fund fees. Results shows: starting value + money added + investment result − spending = ending value, with `investment result = marketChange + jarInterest` summed over the chapter.

### Benchmark plans

Two benchmark plans run on the same market with the same contributions, costs, goal purchases and rates. Both are shown as "Another plan", never as characters.

- **Basket plan:** at month 0, Soon earmark receives the goal amount if the goal is due within 12 months, Buffer receives the chapter's published minimum cost; the rest buys the Basket. Contributions go to the Basket. Reinvest on.
- **Jar plan:** everything stays in the Jar. Contributions go to the Jar.
- **Expense funding order** for both: wallet, then Buffer, then Soon, then a pro-rata sale of holdings. If an expense still cannot be paid, it is recorded as unmet and the comparison is flagged as differing in goal completion.
- **"Change this one choice"** (time machine): earlier actions are frozen; after the divergence point a declared policy applies (hold existing holdings, route contributions per the changed setting, keep goal deadlines). It is labeled "another path", never "what would have happened".

### Determinism and numeric policy

- One `ENGINE_VERSION = 'MS1'`, one `CONTENT_VERSION = 'DECK1'`, one `RUBRIC_VERSION = 'PLAN1'`. A market's identity is `${ENGINE_VERSION}|${CONTENT_VERSION}|${scenarioId}|${seed}`.
- PRNG: cyrb128 seeding into sfc32, four independent streams keyed `|mood`, `|events`, `|prices`, `|cosmetic`. `Math.random` never appears inside `ENGINE`.
- Inside the tick: only `+ − × ÷`, `Math.round`, `Math.floor`, `Math.imul`, `>>>`, BigInt. **No `Math.pow`, `**`, `exp`, `log`, `sqrt`, `sin` or `cos`**: these are implementation-approximated in ECMAScript. Every constant that would need them (√12, each asset's `own`, the Irwin-Hall scale) is a literal in `PARAMS`. Use `x * x`, not `x ** 2`.
- Asset and event iteration order is the fixed order of `PARAMS.assets` and `CONTENT.cards`. No object-key iteration in the engine; use arrays.
- No locale-dependent parsing, sorting or formatting inside the engine. Formatting happens in the UI layer only.
- This is a strong default, not a proof. The proof is the fixture suite in section 13, replayed in Node, Chromium and WebKit.

```js
// One shop, one month. a.own, a.volM, a.driftM are literals or precomputed once at load with + - * / only.
function priceStep(a, pOpen, mood, zM, zS, zO, eventShock, payDividend){
  const g = a.driftM + mood.driftM*(a.mkt/0.5) + a.volM*mood.volMul*(a.mkt*zM + a.sec*zS + a.own*zO) + eventShock;
  const G = Math.max(1 + g, 0.4);
  const D = payDividend && G >= 0.7 ? pOpen*a.yield/4 : 0;   // float; D_cc is rounded from the opening quote
  return { pNext: pOpen*G - D, G, D };
}

const quote = p => Math.max(1, Math.round(p*100));              // float -> cc
const posValue = (msh, q) => Number(BigInt(msh)*BigInt(q)/1000n);

// Look-through exposure for the Spread score: weights by company, funds unwrapped.
function exposure(positions, fund, quotes){
  const w = new Array(6).fill(0); let tot = 0;
  for (const p of positions){
    if (p.id === 'BASKET'){ const nav = posValue(p.msh, quotes.BASKET);
      for (let i=0;i<6;i++) w[i] += nav * fund.weight[i]; tot += nav; }
    else { const v = posValue(p.msh, quotes[p.id]); w[p.idx] += v; tot += v; }
  }
  return tot ? w.map(x => x/tot) : null;   // null = nothing invested, Spread not applicable
}
```

## 7. Plan Practice score

The score measures process at decision time, never outcomes. A child who follows a sound plan into a bad market scores the same as one who follows it into a good market. The child's main view is five descriptive cards; the 0 to 100 number sits behind "Show details". It is a scenario rubric, not a rating of the child, and there is no leaderboard. Rubric version `PLAN1`.

### Dimensions

| Dimension | Weight | Introduced | Evidence counted | Never deducted for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Goal fit | 25 | Ch 1 | At a goal-fit checkpoint, the goal money due within 12 months sits in the Soon earmark, or the child revised the goal (amount or date) before committing | A disappointing return afterwards |
| Readiness | 20 | Ch 3 | At the readiness checkpoint, Buffer holds at least the published minimum of the cost range | Spending the Buffer on the cost it was for |
| Spread | 20 | Ch 2 | After an intentional allocation at a spread checkpoint, look-through exposure of the invested sleeve is at most 40% in one company and 60% in one sector | Holding only cash (not applicable, not zero) |
| Follow-through | 20 | Ch 1 | At a drop or cost checkpoint, the child opens My Plan (or the recap's plan panel) before choosing Buy, Sell, Fund it or Continue | Selling, buying, or keeping the plan; any specific choice |
| Cost awareness | 15 | Ch 6 | In the fee lab, both cost cards were opened before a basket was chosen | Choosing the higher-fee basket |

The 40% and 60% bands are game teaching thresholds, stored as editable scenario constants, and never described as real-world advice.

### Opportunity table

Every opportunity has an ID, fires at most once, and is declared in `CONTENT.chapters[n].opportunities`. Extra taps, repeated screens or more trades never add points.

| Chapter | Goal fit | Readiness | Spread | Follow-through | Cost awareness |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

"Drop stop" means a yearly stop where the invested sleeve's value fell 10% or more since the previous stop. If a chapter produces no drop and no cost, Follow-through is not applicable and is excluded from the average.

### Computation

1. For each introduced dimension with at least one applicable opportunity, `credit = passed ÷ applicable`.
2. Score = weighted average of those credits × 100, rounded to the nearest integer. Weights renormalize over the dimensions that count.
3. Evidence is evaluated when the decision is committed, before the next tick. Future prices, unannounced costs and mood changes are never inputs.
4. Until a chapter ends, the cards say "so far". Opportunities not yet offered are excluded, not zero. Dimensions not yet introduced show "Coming later".
5. If nothing applies yet, show "Exploring" and no number.
6. If a shock makes a goal unaffordable, revising the goal is itself a passing Goal-fit action. Poverty is never graded.
7. Market drift alone never removes Spread credit. Spread is checked only after an intentional allocation at a checkpoint.
8. Scores from different chapters, presets or presentation modes are never compared or summed. Exports carry `rubricVersion`, `scenarioId`, `presentation`.

### Cards and badges

The five cards read as plain observations, each with one sentence of evidence: "Goal fit: you kept 30 coins ready for your art supplies." Badges are earned once per chapter and are never required for story progress: **Checked my plan**, **Compared costs**, **Prepared for a cost**, **Explored a basket**, **Used money for my goal**. The v1 badges Steady Oak and Slow & Steady are removed.

### Abuse tests (must pass in the harness)

- Choosing any reason text or button label never changes the score.
- Opening a cost card 100 times earns the same credit as once.
- Basket plus Bakery counts Bakery exposure once at look-through weight, not as seven holdings.
- A planned withdrawal for the goal never erases earlier Readiness or Goal-fit credit.
- Two identical action sequences on two different seeds earn identical scores.
- A random-clicker bot scores lower than a plan-following bot on the same seed set, and a cash-only bot in chapter 3 can score 100.

## 10. Art inventory and style brief

- `<script id="engine">`: pure. Exposes `globalThis.ENGINE = { createMarket, createRun, validateAction, applyAction, advanceMonth, summarizeRun, scorePlanPractice, replaySave, comparePlan, VERSION }`. No DOM, timers, `Date`, `Math.random`, `fetch` or `localStorage` references. `tools/test.mjs` greps for these and fails if any appear.
- `<script id="content">`: `PARAMS` (assets, moods), `CONTENT` (chapters, cards, presets, opportunities), `STRINGS`. Data only.
- `<script id="app">`: store (`state`, `dispatch` → reducer → debounced save → render), screens, chart renderer, persistence, PWA registration, settings, speech.

```js
createMarket({ engineVersion, contentVersion, scenarioId, seed })      // -> market: prices, moods, index, scheduled cards
createRun({ scenarioId, marketId, presentation, initialPlan })         // -> state
validateAction(state, action)   // -> { ok:true, preview } | { ok:false, code, detail }; never mutates
applyAction(state, action)      // -> new state; pure
advanceMonth(state, market)     // -> { state, stopped:boolean, queue:[cards] }  one tick, section 5 order
summarizeRun(state)             // -> ledger totals, panels, chart series
scorePlanPractice(state, rubric) // -> { cards, total|null, opportunities }
replaySave(save)                // -> state rebuilt from checkpoint + actions
comparePlan(save, policy)       // -> benchmark or one-change path on the same market
```

Actions are plain objects with a `seq` number: `{ seq, t, type:'BUY'|'SELL'|'JAR_IN'|'JAR_OUT'|'EARMARK'|'ROUTE'|'REINVEST'|'GOAL_REVISE'|'FUND_COST'|'POSTPONE'|'ADVANCE'|'CHOOSE_BASKET'|'DECORATE', ... }`. A repeated `seq` is ignored, which makes double taps and retried dispatches idempotent.

### Save design

Versioned, with a validated checkpoint plus the actions since it. Replay from month 0 is a test tool, not the load path.

```js
{ schemaVersion:1, engineVersion:'MS1', contentVersion:'DECK1', rubricVersion:'PLAN1',
  runId:'r_8f3k2', scenarioId:'ch3', seed:'ch3|a9f2c1', tick:18, lastSeq:32,
  checkpoint:{ tick:12, wallet:4120, jar:{soon:8000, buffer:1500}, positions:[{id:'BAKE', msh:3000}], fund:{...},
               goal:{...}, totals:{...}, opportunities:[...], moodState:..., index:1.0 },
  actionsSinceCheckpoint:[ {seq:31, t:13, type:'BUY', id:'BASKET', cc:1000}, ... ],
  pending:[ /* actions not yet committed by an ADVANCE; Undo pops here */ ],
  options:{ presentation:'guided', reducedMotion:false, sound:false, textSize:1, voice:null },
  progress:{ completedChapters:['ch1','ch2'], decorations:{ch1:'plants-2'}, scrapbook:[...] } }
```

- Keys: `ms:save` (current), `ms:save:bak` (last known good, rotated on every successful save), `ms:save:bad` (first corrupt copy, never overwritten by later failures), `ms:settings`, `ms:owner` (tab lock: random tab id plus timestamp, refreshed every 5 seconds, plus a `BroadcastChannel('mainstreet')` message on takeover).
- Save after every committed action and chapter transition. Wrap every storage call in try/catch. On failure show the banner from section 8 and offer Export.
- `runId` is random, local, never transmitted, and carries no device information.
- Migrations: `while (save.schemaVersion < CURRENT) save = MIGRATE[save.schemaVersion](save)`. If `engineVersion` cannot be replayed, offer a read-only Results view and Export; never silently recompute history with a different engine.
- Export/Import: a JSON file via `<a download>` and `<input type="file">`. Import validates schema, size (under 256 KB), numeric ranges and versions, and treats content strictly as data (no HTML insertion, no `eval`, no prototype keys). Import never overwrites the current save without a confirm; it lands in a new slot first.
- Browser storage can be evicted. The Grown-ups page says so and points to Export.

### PWA and service worker

- `sw.js`: cache name `mainstreet-${CONTENT_VERSION}-${ENGINE_VERSION}`. Precache `./`, `./index.html`, `./manifest.webmanifest`, both icons. Cache-first for those; everything else is not requested. On activate, delete only caches whose name starts with `mainstreet-`.
- Serve `sw.js` with `Cache-Control: no-cache`. The new worker waits; the page shows "A new version is ready" on Home only and calls `skipWaiting` on the user's tap after a save. Never reload mid-advance.
- The first visit needs a connection. After the install event completes, the full game must work in airplane mode; this is a release test.
- No `blob:` service worker or `data:` manifest. Both break installation on Android and iOS.

### Privacy boundaries

Gameplay issues no API, telemetry, ad, font, image or speech requests. Read-aloud uses `speechSynthesis` only with voices where `localService === true`; otherwise the button is hidden. No third-party script of any kind. Hosting logs belong to the host and are outside the game; the Grown-ups page says "this game sends nothing" and does not claim the host collects nothing. A future store wrapper needs its own review before any "no data collected" label.

### Performance budgets

| Measure | Target | How measured |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `index.html` size | under 150 KB uncompressed (prototype budget; see section 10) | `wc -c`, reported in `STATUS.md` |
| Total precached package | under 400 KB | sum of precache files |
| Market precompute, 240 months | under 100 ms on the reference device | `performance.now()` in the debug panel |
| Control response | under 100 ms tap to visible change | manual with DevTools performance on the reference device |
| Year advance animation | no frame longer than 50 ms; skippable | DevTools |
| Idle | no animation loop while the page is static | `requestAnimationFrame` only during playback |

Prices in a `Float64Array` per asset; quotes in an `Int32Array`. Canvas for the chart with `devicePixelRatio` capped at 2 and a table fallback; DOM/SVG for everything interactive. No CSS filters or large shadows.

### Browser support

Chrome and Edge on Android and desktop (last two major versions), Safari on iOS 16 and later, Firefox desktop current. BigInt, `BroadcastChannel`, `speechSynthesis` and service workers are present in all of these.

## 13. Acceptance tests

Every test below is automated in `tools/test.mjs` unless marked manual. A milestone passes only when its tests pass and `STATUS.md` records the run.

### Financial correctness

| Test | Required result |
| --- | --- |
| Buy then sell at an unchanged quote | Net worth unchanged within 1 cc per transaction; 1,000 repeated cycles lose under 1,000 cc and never gain |
| Dividend with G = 1 | Position value after plus cash paid equals position value before, within 1 cc per holder |
| Reinvest on | Cash paid converts to shares once at the ex-dividend quote; `contributionsIn` unchanged |
| Basket internal dividends | Basket NAV path with dividends equals the sum of its holdings' total-return paths times the fee factor; no double count |
| Fund plus stock look-through | Basket plus Bakery exposure equals Bakery weight in the fund plus the direct holding, computed once |
| Matched fee lab | Identical holdings and flows; Basket B never finishes above Basket A; the difference equals the compounded fee gap within 1 cc |
| Wallet and Jar transfers | Net worth and `contributionsIn` unchanged |
| Goal purchase | Cash falls once, `goalSpending` rises once, score unchanged by the purchase |
| Contribution month | Net worth rises by exactly the contribution before market change; recap labels it as money added |
| Overspend, oversell, zero quote, negative amount | `validateAction` returns an error; state untouched; no NaN, Infinity or negative balance |
| Closure | Trading suspended at announcement; liquidation paid once next month; holding removed; Basket redistributes without fee; no positive price floor keeps the shop alive |
| Benchmark expense funding | Both benchmarks pay the same costs in the stated order; an unmet cost is reported, never dropped |
| Zero or near-zero wealth | Results, chart and score render; no division by zero |
| Money identity | Holds at every tick on every fixture |
| Undo | Restores the exact prior state; Undo after an advance is impossible |

### Determinism

- Eight golden fixtures: chapter 1 script; chapters 2 to 6 with one seed each; free play Standard 240 months; free play Shops Can Close. Each stores the full quote series hash, the mood path, the scheduled cards and the final ledger totals for the Basket plan.
- `tools/replay.html` loads the same engine script and prints PASS/FAIL per fixture. Run in Node, Chromium and WebKit via Playwright. Any mismatch fails M1.
- Purity grep: the engine script contains none of `Math.random`, `Date`, `document`, `window`, `localStorage`, `fetch`, `setTimeout`, `**`, `Math.pow`, `Math.exp`, `Math.log`, `Math.sqrt`, `Math.sin`, `Math.cos`, `toLocale`.
- Replaying the same action log twice yields byte-identical saves.
- Animation speed, reduced motion, screen switching and reading time never change any ledger value (test by driving the store with and without rendering).

### Interaction and recovery (manual, recorded in `docs/playtest.md`)

- Refresh mid-chapter, app kill, airplane mode after install, blocked storage (private mode), corrupt import file, duplicate tap on Confirm, two tabs on one run, update arriving mid-run: each follows its section 8 state and loses no committed action.
- No market advance while the page is hidden or closed; no repeated dividend on reopen.
- Keyboard only and TalkBack: complete chapter 1, execute and cancel a trade, open and leave every screen.
- Network panel during a full chapter after install: zero requests.
- Import of a crafted JSON with HTML strings, `__proto__` keys and oversized numbers: rejected or sanitized; nothing renders as markup.

### Calibration report (`docs/calibration.md`, M6)

Run the finished engine, with cards, contributions and the final scoring, over at least 10,000 seeds per scenario family. Tune on one seed set; report on a held-out set. Report the engine, content and rubric versions, the seed rule, sample sizes, and execution time on the reference device. For each strategy policy (Basket plan, Jar plan, single-shop hold, panic seller, random clicker, plan follower) report nominal and real ending wealth at p10, p50 and p90, the share of runs that lost to the index, maximum drawdown, goal fulfillment rate, and the Plan Practice distribution. Keep no-contribution runs separate from contribution runs, and report investment gain from the ledger rather than final divided by initial wealth.

The report describes the toy world. It is not forced to hit v1's targets. It must show: some single-shop runs beating the Basket; some Basket runs losing money in real terms; cash-only succeeding at short-horizon goals in chapter 3; the high-fee basket never beating the low-fee basket on identical holdings; and playable, understandable results under each stress preset (Early Storm, Late Storm, Long Quiet, Rising Prices, Savings Pay Well, Shops Can Close). If any asset's realized drift is more than 0.5 points from its parameter after event correction, adjust the parameter, record it, and rerun.

## 14. Playtest checklist and release blockers

Penny is the first tester, not the proof. Follow with five to eight children across the reading range, with a grown-up's permission, no recordings and no analytics. Observe first; explain nothing until you have seen where the game fails. Write anonymous notes about actions and misconceptions only. This is formative usability work, not an education study, and no public copy should claim learning outcomes from it.

### Prompts and what success looks like

| Prompt or moment | Success |
| --- | --- |
| "What did you buy?" | A small piece or a share, not the whole shop or its muffins |
| "What money can you use soon?" | Points to the wallet or Jar, not the shares |
| A price drop appears | Does not tap faster, hunt for a button to hold, or sell by reflex |
| "What if this shop closes?" | Knows the Basket still holds the others and can still lose |
| A practice contribution arrives | Can say the money was added, not earned by the shares |
| Dividend screen | Describes a payment or buying more shares, not a tapping bonus |
| "What if you need your goal money now?" | Names the Jar or a planned sale as reasonable |
| Fee lab | Can say costs leave less, without calling fees a trick |
| A disappointing chapter ends | Names one decision they would keep and stops satisfied |
| Leave and come back | Finds the same saved decision and knows how to continue |
| "What would you change next time?" | Suggests a plan change, not a prediction of a winning shop |

### Release blockers

Any of these observed in two or more testers blocks release until fixed:

- [ ] Belief that losses always recover
- [ ] Belief that cash is always foolish, or that every sale is bad
- [ ] Belief that decorations or chapter progress improve returns
- [ ] Belief that more trading earns more reward
- [ ] Belief that a share buys the whole shop
- [ ] Any mention of real crypto, real stocks or "the game says I should buy"
- [ ] An accidental trade (confirm pressed without intent)
- [ ] A control unreachable by keyboard or TalkBack on the main path
- [ ] A total on Results that does not reconcile with the chart or the ledger
- [ ] A routine save failure that loses a committed action

### Product gates (report counts, not conclusions)

- Most first-time testers finish the chapter 1 opening with at most one navigation hint.
- Most testers can explain ownership and the purpose of the Jar after chapter 3.
- Several testers voluntarily try a second chapter or a time machine experiment.
- A delayed retest on a new seed checks transfer, not memory of a button sequence.

### Known limitations (to appear in the Grown-ups page and release notes)

- The market is a toy. Bounded noise, three moods and six companies cannot reproduce real market behavior; stress presets stand in for rare events.
- The Town Basket is six shops in one town, not a broad index.
- No taxes, debt, bonds, leverage, limit orders, spreads, liquidity or real prices.
- The score measures planning habits inside this game. It does not validate real-world financial behavior.
- Browser storage can be cleared or evicted; Export is the backup.
- The first visit needs a connection; everything after that works offline.
- Read-aloud depends on voices installed on the device.
