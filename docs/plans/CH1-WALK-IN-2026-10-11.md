# Plan: the walk in (R17), chapter 1 as a guided walk, 2026-10-11

Stephen, 21:20 UTC, after rc7: "this first level is still just stupid ... The tutorial needs to be like the person walking in and being like, here's your hundred coins, you're going to put them here, and then you're going to try and save up over the year by investing in these businesses, you're trying to put the coins for your goal here, these are how you allocate them into businesses ... we have My Plan but it doesn't tell us what it is or what it's for ... you're going to be clicking to go through time, it has no explanation as to what the heck we're doing."

He is right about the shape: the rc4 guide explained each stop, never the screen. Nothing ever pointed at the money row, the plan tray, My Plan or the time button and said what it is. Chapter 1 becomes a guided walk: the Town Clerk lights one piece of the screen at a time, says what it is in one or two lines, and the player does the step. Every chapter 1 player walks it the first time. No engine change, no content numbers.

## 1. The spotlight

A walk layer rendered after the Town screen when `S.ui.walk` is a step index (chapter 1 only; `null` elsewhere; saved with the run next to `tutorialStep`, which it replaces for chapter 1).

- A full screen blocker (`div.walk-block`, `position: fixed; inset: 0`) with a dark tint (`rgba(43,36,32,.55)`) and a hole over the target: `clip-path: polygon(evenodd, 0 0, 100% 0, 100% 100%, 0 100%, 0 0, x1 y1, x1 y2, x2 y2, x2 y1, x1 y1)` from the target's bounding rect with 8 px padding. Taps in the hole reach the real control; taps outside do nothing. A `div.walk-ring` (`position: fixed`, 3 px border in `--up`, radius 12) sits on the same rect, `pointer-events: none`.
- The target is scrolled into view first (`scrollIntoView({ block: 'center' })`), then measured; the walk re-measures on `scroll` and `resize` (rAF throttled) and after every `render()`.
- The Clerk's bubble (`section.walk-card`, `position: fixed`, left and right 12 px, below the target when the target is in the top half, above it otherwise, never covering it): the eyebrow "The Town Clerk", the text, and either a **Next** button (48 px) or the required action's button, or no button when the required action is a tap on the lit control (then the text ends with what to tap).
- The bubble and the pinned footer (`.town-foot`) must not cover each other: when the target is the footer or inside it, the bubble sits above the footer.
- Reduced motion changes nothing (there is no animation). Keyboard: the bubble's button is focused when a step opens; the lit control is reachable by Tab.
- Returning players: when `S.progress.completedChapters` includes `ch1`, the bubble shows a small "Skip the walk" link that sets the walk to done. First timers have no skip.

## 2. The steps (copy is final; the Clerk speaks)

Intro screen (`introScreen`), replacing the current Clerk card and the practice line:

> "Welcome to {town}. I am the Town Clerk. One shop is open this year, Bram's bakery, and you have a year to learn how owning a piece of it works. Come in, I will show you around." Button: **Come in**.

On Town, in order (target in brackets; "Next" means the bubble has a Next button; "do" means the step ends when the player does the thing):

1. [the money row] "Here are your coins: 100, all pretend. Wallet is what you can spend right now. Jar is money you keep safe. Spent is what you have paid for so far." Next.
2. [the goal chip] "This year you are saving for one thing: art supplies, 30 coins, due next spring. The bar fills as your Jar fills." Next.
3. [the Jar box] "Goal money goes in the Jar first, so a quiet month at the bakery cannot touch it. Put 30 in now." Bubble button **Put 30 in my Jar** (queues `JAR_IN` soon 3000 as today). Then, same target: "30 is safe. The other 70 are free to try growing." Next.
4. [the bakery tile] "This is the bakery. A business is split into tiny pieces called shares. Own one and you own a tiny piece of the shop. Tap the bakery." do: tap the tile (opens the panel).
5. [the share card: price, You own, the steppers] "A share costs 10 right now. Plus buys one, minus sells one. Try one, three or seven. More shares means more of your money moves with the price." do: tap plus at least once. (The steppers must be enabled from this step; today they unlock at `tutorialStep >= 4`.)
6. [the plan tray in the footer] "That bought nothing yet. It went into your plan, here at the bottom. A plan is what you will do before time moves. Undo takes a line back. Nothing is spent until you say go." Next.
7. [the My Plan button] "My Plan shows the shape of your money: what is in the Jar, what is in shares, what is still free. Open it." do: tap My Plan. On the plan screen a Clerk card at the top (all chapters keep this card afterwards, see section 3) and the walk waits; its **Back to town** returns to step 8.
8. [the Next season button] "Time moves only when you tap Next season. Three months pass. The bakery has busy days and quiet days and its price moves. Your shares move with it. Your Jar does not. Tap it when you are ready." do: tap Next season.
9. [the confirm sheet, whole] "The last look before time moves: your plan in words, then the two ways it could go. Tap Go to spring." do: tap Go. The walk ends; the season show, the recap and the rc4 stop guides follow. The stop guides at 3, 6 and 9 months keep their text but carry the Clerk's eyebrow and border instead of "Practice year".

Chapter 1's Next season button stays disabled until step 8, as it is today before the share is queued.

## 3. My Plan explains itself (every chapter)

At the top of the plan screen, a Clerk card: "Your plan is the shape of your money. Soon is money for your goal. Buffer is for surprises. Saved is extra you set aside. Shares are the pieces of shops you own. Move coins between them here any time before time moves; nothing is spent until you confirm." Buffer and Saved lines appear only in chapters that have those bands.
